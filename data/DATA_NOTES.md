# Data Sources & Methodology

The datasets in this folder (`messi_club_stats.csv`, `messi_international_stats.csv`)
were compiled from publicly available career statistics (ESPN, Sofascore, FotMob,
Transfermarkt-style season archives) as of **September 2026**, shortly after Lionel
Messi's announced retirement from international football with Argentina
(August 31, 2026). He continues his club career with Inter Miami CF (MLS) through
his contract, which runs to 2028.

Notes:
- "All competitions" appearance/goal/assist figures combine league, continental,
  and domestic cup competitions for each season.
- 2026 club and international figures reflect the season through his final
  international appearance at the 2026 FIFA World Cup; the Inter Miami 2026
  season figures are as of early September 2026 and may not reflect the final
  MLS matches of the year.
- `messi_international_stats.csv` covers **major tournaments only** (World Cups,
  Copa América) — it excludes friendlies and qualifiers, so its goal/assist totals
  are a subset of his full international record, not his all-time national-team tally.
- Figures are aggregated for portfolio/analytics-practice purposes. Minor
  discrepancies of a few goals/assists/appearances exist across public sources
  (ESPN vs Sofascore vs Transfermarkt) due to differing treatment of shootouts,
  friendlies, and cup qualifiers — this dataset uses the most commonly cited
  values and is not intended as an official record.
